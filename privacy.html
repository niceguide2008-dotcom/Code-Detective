Privacy Policy

How Code Detective may handle information needed to provide its learning experience.

Last updated: August 16, 2026
Important: This page describes the intended privacy practices of the current Code Detective learning application. It is not legal advice. Before public or commercial launch, replace any project-specific placeholders and have the policy reviewed for the jurisdictions in which the service operates.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Code Detective may process account information such as a display name and email address, learning information such as completed challenges and XP, and technical information required to operate and secure the service.

2. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • To save learning progress, achievements, XP, and challenge history.
  • To provide requested learning features and progress displays.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the platform.

3. Data storage and service providers

The application uses third-party infrastructure for parts of its functionality, including hosted application data and authentication. The deployed production policy should identify the actual providers, processing purposes, and applicable contractual safeguards.

4. Browser storage

Code Detective may use browser or device storage where necessary to support application functionality, preferences, sessions, or similar features. If analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies are introduced, this policy should be updated before those features are enabled.

5. Data sharing

Personal information is not intended to be sold. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the application, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or its rights.

6. Data retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to security, operational, and legal requirements.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other handling of personal information. The production version should provide a real contact method for privacy requests.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Code Detective should comply with applicable age-related privacy requirements. If the service is directed to children or knowingly collects children's information, obtain appropriate legal guidance and update this policy before launch.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

11. Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official contact channel published by the Code Detective project owner.
